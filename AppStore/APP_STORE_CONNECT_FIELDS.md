### Description (4000)
```
Aligner Tracker helps clear-aligner and retainer wearers stay on track and finish treatment on time. It's simple, private, and works completely offline.

WEAR-TIME TIMER
• One big tap to start or pause wear tracking
• Live session timer plus today's running total
• A smooth circular progress ring toward your daily goal (default 22 hours)
• Keeps counting accurately even if you close the app, and correctly splits time across midnight

DAILY LOG & STREAKS
• Color-coded calendar showing every day's wear time at a glance — green (goal met), yellow (close), red (missed)
• Weekly and monthly averages
• A streak counter for consecutive days that hit your goal

ALIGNER CHANGE CYCLE
• Set your current tray, total trays, and change interval (typically 7–14 days)
• See the days remaining and a countdown to your next change right on the home screen
• Local reminders the day before and on change day — each at a time you choose

PHOTO DIARY
• Snap a smile photo every time you switch trays
• Each entry is auto-stamped with the date, tray number, and treatment day
• Browse a timeline gallery, open any entry full screen, and export as PDF

WIDGETS
• Home Screen and Lock Screen widgets show today's wear time, your tray number, and days until the next change — without opening the app

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
• 100% offline — no account, no ads, no tracking
• All data stays on your device; export anytime as JSON
• Full English and Simplified Chinese support

Start tracking today and give your smile the consistency it needs.
```
