# Privacy Policy — Aligner Tracker

_Last updated: 2026-06-02_

Aligner Tracker ("the app") is designed to be completely private. This policy
explains what the app does and does not do with your information.

## Summary

**Aligner Tracker does not collect, transmit, sell, or share any personal data.**
The app works fully offline. There is no account, no analytics, and no backend
server.

## What data the app stores

All data you create stays **on your device** (and in your private iCloud device
backup, if you have iOS backups enabled). This includes:

- Wear-time sessions and daily totals
- Aligner tray number, change interval, and schedule
- Photo-diary entries (photos and notes you add)
- App settings (daily goal, notification preferences, appearance)

This data is stored locally using Apple's on-device storage (SwiftData and an
App Group shared with the app's widget). None of it leaves your device unless
**you** choose to share it — for example, by using the "Export data (JSON)"
button, the PDF/photo share options, or your own device backup.

## Camera and photo library

If you choose to add a photo to your diary, the app accesses the camera or your
photo library only at that moment and only to save the image you select into the
app's local diary. Photos are not uploaded anywhere.

## Notifications

The app schedules **local** notifications (aligner-change reminders and an
optional daily wear reminder) directly on your device. No notification data is
sent to any server.

## Data collected by Apple

Aligner Tracker itself collects nothing. If you download the app from the App
Store, Apple may collect information as described in Apple's own privacy policy.

## Children

The app does not collect data from anyone, including children.

## Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at the URL where you
