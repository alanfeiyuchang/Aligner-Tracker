## Description
Aligner Tracker helps clear-aligner and retainer wearers stay on track and
finish treatment on time.

WEAR-TIME TIMER
• One big tap to start or pause wear tracking
• Live session timer and today's total
• Circular progress ring toward your daily goal (default 22 hours)
• Keeps counting accurately even if you close the app or cross midnight

DAILY LOG & STREAKS
• Color-coded calendar of every day's wear time
• Weekly and monthly averages
• Streak counter for consecutive days that meet your goal

ALIGNER CHANGE CYCLE
• Set your current tray, total trays, and change interval
• See days remaining and a countdown to your next change
• Local reminders the day before and on change day — each at a time you choose

PHOTO DIARY
• Snap a smile photo every time you switch trays
• Auto-stamped with date, tray number, and treatment day
• Timeline gallery, full-screen detail, and PDF export

WIDGETS
• Home Screen and Lock Screen widgets show today's wear time, tray number,
  and days until your next change at a glance

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
• 100% offline — no account, no ads, no tracking
• All data stays on your device; export anytime as JSON
• Full English and Simplified Chinese support
